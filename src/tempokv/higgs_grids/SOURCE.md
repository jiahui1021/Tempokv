These two-dimensional HIGGS codebooks come from AQUA-KV commit
`e744183f2f3a4a90381f20a6975456871fd1ae29` at
https://github.com/goodevening13/aquakv. See the accompanying Apache-2.0 LICENSE.

`gaussian_scalar.json` contains symmetric one-dimensional standard-normal
Lloyd–Max grids for 1 and 5–8 bits. The stored points satisfy the conditional
mean equations for cells with midpoint boundaries, solved in float64 and
rounded to nine decimal places. These are TempoKV extension grids.
